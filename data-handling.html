Data & Transparency

How we use and handle your data.

At SHUCHI, data handling is simple, secure, and fully transparent. Here is exactly what we store, why we store it, and how your privacy is protected.

1. What Data We Store

We collect and store only the minimal information required to complete your checkout and manage your customer account. This is strictly limited to:

  • Full Name: To address your delivery packages.
  • Phone Number: To contact you for order verification and login authentication (no passwords required).
  • Delivery Address: To deliver your skincare products to your home or office.

We do not request, store, or process any payment credentials, passwords, or secondary personal identifiers.

2. Why We Store It

Your data is stored solely to improve your shopping experience and manage logistics:

  • Ease of Checkout: When you sign in with your phone number, your default name and address automatically pre-fill on the delivery details form so you don't have to type them every time.
  • Purchase History: Logging in allows you to securely view your past order records, delivery status, and order IDs.
  • Logistics: To coordinate physical shipping within Dhaka and outer districts in Bangladesh.

3. How Cookies Are Used

Cookies are tiny text files stored in your web browser. SHUCHI uses cookies for structural functionality only:

  • Session Persistence (`shuchi_user`): We set a secure cookie containing your name, phone, and address once you authenticate. This tells our website that you are logged in and keeps you signed in across pages.
  • Bag State Storage (`shuchi_cart`): We utilize standard browser local storage to remember what skincare products are currently inside your shopping bag, even if you close the tab.

We do not use third-party marketing, analytics, profiling, or tracking cookies of any kind.

Our Privacy Promise

"We treat your information with the same honesty we apply to our skincare curation. We will never sell, lease, share, or monetize your phone number, address, or purchase history with third-party advertising companies. Your records are private and securely stored inside the Shuchi User database."
